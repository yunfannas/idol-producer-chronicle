## Code-path finding

Existing-O eligibility is reconstructed per member from the same event-local attending O: a member's temporary affinity sample is removed, then actual member exposure selects the remainder. Samples are independent by member, so fans of member j remain eligible for i and one temporary attendee may form multiple A edges. `share` is the authoritative per-show exposure share from the executable formula, not a mutually-exclusive fan allocation; it bounds the total Live affinity hazard and was retained.

The missing path was completed existing-O Cheki trial to a new member A. The repair adds a real ¥1,000 trial request only for exposed, unattached, stayed O; it shares the normal queue and is eligible for A only after completed service. Live and Cheki hazards for the same member/contact are mutually combined.
